Direct call

Peer-to-peer video call with no server in between. You and your friend swap two short codes over any messaging app, and the call connects directly.

Create your invitation

Your browser will ask for camera and microphone access. The invitation contains your IP addresses.

Paste your friend’s reply

Send the invitation to your friend. They will send you a reply code back.

Paste the invitation

Paste the code your friend sent you. Your browser will ask for camera and microphone access.

Send your reply

Send this code back to your friend. The call starts when they connect.

When does this work?
  • Without STUN or TURN servers, the call can only use the addresses found on your own devices. Both of you must be able to reach each other directly.
  • Same Wi-Fi or LAN: works out of the box.
  • Over the internet: works if both of you have a public IPv6 address, or if you share a VPN or overlay network such as Tailscale, ZeroTier or WireGuard.
  • Two different home networks on IPv4: usually fails, because NAT blocks incoming connections and there is no server to help get through it.
  • Each invitation and reply works once. For a new call, start over.
